Muchenjeri Journal

A personal, offline-first journal for Android.

What it is

Muchenjeri Journal is a private journal app for a single person's own use. It is not a commercial product and is not distributed on app stores. Entries, photos and voice notes are saved on the phone first, so the journal works fully without an internet connection.

Why it uses Google Drive

If you connect a Google account, the app backs up the journal to a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive, so it can be restored after reinstalling or on a new phone. The app requests only the drive.appdata permission, which lets it see the folder it creates and nothing else in your Drive.

Privacy

There is no server, analytics, advertising or tracking. Journal content goes only to your phone and your own Google Drive. Read the full privacy policy.

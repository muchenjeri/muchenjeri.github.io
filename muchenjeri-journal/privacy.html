Privacy Policy

Muchenjeri Journal · Effective 12 October 2026

Muchenjeri Journal ("the app") is a personal journal app for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data the app stores

The journal entries you write (titles, text, dates), photos you attach, and voice notes you record. This data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by Android's standard app sandboxing.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect a Google account, the app copies your journal to a hidden application-data folder in your own Google Drive so it can be restored later. The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope, which allows it to create, read and delete files in its own app-data folder. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive. The app also uses your email address only to show which account is connected.

You can disconnect at any time in the app's Settings. To delete the backed-up copy, open Google Drive, go to Settings › Manage apps, and choose "Delete hidden app data" for Muchenjeri Journal.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send journal content to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. There is no such server.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • It does not use your data for advertising or to train machine-learning models.

Speech recognition

The dictation feature uses Android's built-in speech recognition. When your phone supports on-device recognition, audio stays on the phone. Otherwise, the speech service on your phone (usually Google's) may process the audio under its own privacy policy. Voice notes are recordings only and are never transcribed or uploaded anywhere other than your own Google Drive backup.

Exports

If you use "Export journal", the app writes a ZIP file to the location you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is under your control.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
